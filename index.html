How the columns are computed
  • Deal: links to the store's product page when the post links to one (affiliate and tracking codes removed); otherwise to the post. Source: links to the post.
  • Price: the price in the post's title (for a fare, the lowest fare listed). Usual price: the regular, list, pre-coupon, or earlier selling price the post states; with only a percentage off stated, the price that percentage implies; with only a saving stated (e.g., "a $14 low"), the price plus that saving. Blank when the post states none. Only posts naming one product (an item or a fare) at one price are listed; store-wide sales, roundups, promotions, and card or bank bonuses are left out.
  • Discount: from the post's own numbers ("40% off", "was $299", "to $81 from $88", "a $39 savings"). An error fare with no usual fare stated is set to 60%. A blank means the post states no usual price.
  • Honor chance: a starting estimate by seller (airline 55%, Costco 50%, eBay 40%, Amazon 30%, hotel 30%, Best Buy 20%, other 30%), plus 30 points when the post reports orders shipped or honored and minus 20 when it reports cancellations.
  • Score: discount times honor chance, with a blank discount counted as 50%.
  • Price error tag: the title or post says price error, pricing error, price mistake, error fare, mistake fare, or glitch.
  • Category: keyword rules over the title; a fare feed's posts are all Travel.
